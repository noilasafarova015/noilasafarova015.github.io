About Me

I am a law graduate from Uzbekistan with a Bachelor's in Jurisprudence and a first-year master's in International and European Law at the Catholic University of Lille, funded by the France Excellence Scholarship. My work focuses on how law can protect people and the environment, especially in Central Asia, where weak enforcement and limited legal capacity allow serious harm such as illegal tree felling, dust storms and air pollution.

I have experience in legal research, memo drafting, moot court advocacy and community legal education, and I plan to continue my master's in 2027/2028.
